Invited Talks & Presentations

  • SAWC Spring | WHS 2025, May 2025, Grapevine, TX — Poster Presenter
    Quantitative Photoacoustic Feature Analysis for Vascular Monitoring in Foot Ulcers
    Presented quantitative feature analysis using 44 features extracted from photoacoustic images, achieving 83.33% classification accuracy and 93.78% macro-AUC for healing, worsening, and healthy outcomes.
  • RCBU Biomedical Ultrasound Symposium, November 2025, Rochester, NY — Oral Presenter
    Wound Healing and Tissue Perfusion Assessed by Photoacoustic Imaging
    Presented longitudinal photoacoustic imaging for chronic foot ulcers in peripheral vascular disease. Our noninvasive method offers tracking of vascular dynamics with enhanced visualization through expanded field of view and skin-artifact suppression. Multi-seed cross-validation yielded 83.3% accuracy and 93.78% macro-AUC classifying healing, worsening, and healthy outcomes.
